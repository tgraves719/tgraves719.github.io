Because we perceive color in 3 bands, we need to build our color wheel into thirds. This is truly an arbitrary cut into the color spectrum, and the only reason RGB was chosen is due to the RGB color model that all virtual devices use to generate light.

When all interval vectors are built off of a tonic with any arbitrary color or note, it will reveal a color pattern and intervallic relationships that are present in all 12 permutations. This concept is defined as “12-Node Theory” which combines set theory, music theory, and color theory to find aesthetic or interesting intervallic relationships to build contrast, harmony, and dissonance.

In any application of audiovisual experiences, the audience’s emotion is what will ultimately connect what they see and hear with what they experience, and the cultural associations with certain sounds and sights will be much stronger in creating specific moods than purely mathematical or geometric associations.
