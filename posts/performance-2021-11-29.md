Scene from our first performance of the beta! This live performance of mixed reality and music features a **brass quintet** playing a piece by Handel. Instead of using real fireworks and risking burning down a building like the original performance, we can use AR to capture a similar effect. Each instrument controls MIDI-driven visual effects with various music equipment.
