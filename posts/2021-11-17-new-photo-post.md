Hi everyone! Its been a long time coming, but the first public performance of my trumpet recital and music technology project Portraits of Change will be on November 28th at 5pm. It will be at Hoag Hall at CSU-Pueblo. DM me if you have any questions. Free admission, please be vaccinated. Will be recorded and possibly live streamed. We will feature 12 musical song environments and 12 audience members will wear an AR headset and watch a real-time audiovisual music performance.
