Very excited for the future of Mixed Reality. Our current Beta prototype uses a PC-VR headset with a 3D printed mount and a depth-camera mounted to the front. This solution is expensive and not very effective to scale with multiple audience members. There are standalone devices like the HoloLens 2 but are more expensive. Then comes in this beautiful piece of tech, the Lynx R-1. This standalone VR/AR headset has built-in cameras for AR passthrough and runs on the same chipset as the Quest 2. There will be a kickstarter for the headset in September and we will absolutely get one for Portraits. At just a few hundred dollars, this option is vastly cheaper and much higher fidelity (both resolution, framerate and FOV) than our beta prototype. My recital will still use the beta hardware, but future performances in 2022 and beyond will feature more MR participants and better visuals with a device like this!
