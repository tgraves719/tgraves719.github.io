Portraits of Change is a mixed-reality jazz show. Learn more at https://portraitsofchange.org/ A recent update to our software includes HDRP support and a look into digital sheet music, callibrating vfx on a piano using hand tracking thanks to the Leap Motion, and playing the piano in a AR environment using VR passthrough with the ZED mini and Valve Index. See the full 0.5.2 preview here- https://youtu.be/RUBBKnUVU-s
