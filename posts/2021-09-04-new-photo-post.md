Hard to believe this was 3D printed! Using HP Multi Jet Fusion 3D-printing technology, I was able to print this camera mount with nylon and print it in color to display our logo! I will spend some time calibrating the camera with the VR headset, but will be able to record more tests with more instruments and get the final pieces in motion for the first public performance. The recital date will be announced this upcoming week! Special thanks to Resin Foundry for making the part quickly and with some crazy cool technology. If anyone in the Springs needs to 3D print with SLA or MJF tech, hit them up! Also, If anyone needs normal stuff 3D printed with FDM, I recommend Catz Design Farm who gave me the referral to Resin Foundry when they couldn't print this part with traditional 3D printing. https://www.resinfoundry.com/ https://www.catzdesignfarm.com/
