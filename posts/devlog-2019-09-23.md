Testing an **electric trumpet** performance in *Portraits of Change 0.2.4*. This experiment pairs Miles Davis’s *Blue in Green* with a matching **blue and green color palette**, performed while orbiting a blue and green planet.  

This test was built using the **Oculus CV1**, a **green screen**, a **Logitech Brio camera**, and **LIV mixed reality software**.
